# Daily Operations

## Overview
Day-to-day warehouse operations and procedures.

## Receiving Process
1. Receive parcels from suppliers
2. Scan and verify
3. Check for damages
4. Update inventory

## Sorting Process
1. Sort by destination
2. Assign to couriers
3. Generate QR codes
4. Prepare for dispatch

## Dispatching Process
1. Verify parcels
2. Assign drivers
3. Generate manifests
4. Track deliveries

## Operating Hours
- Peak Hours: 10 AM - 12 PM
- All sorting areas: 4/4 active

- Barcode Scanners: 3 units
- Duffle Bags: 8 units
- Packing Tape: 5 rolls
